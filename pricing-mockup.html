  • Tennessee property intake and address confirmation
  • Onboarding disclosures and guided questionnaire
  • Tennessee offer documents based on your 1:1 broker call

Questions

What is launch tier pricing?

Launch tiers fill in order: Founding, Level 2, Level 3, then Standard. Prices are limited and subject to availability. The price shown at checkout is the price that applies to your purchase.

How fast can I get an offer out?

Usually about a day. Most buyers are ready to send an offer within 24 hours, depending on property complexity and timely inputs.

How does the flat fee compare to a traditional commission?

Illustrative example: a hypothetical 3% buyer-side fee on a $500K home is $15,000. The recommended bundle at Standard is $3,490. Buyer-side compensation is negotiable; savings are not guaranteed.

Where is BuyUnrepped available?

Early access: Middle Tennessee — Nashville, Franklin, Murfreesboro, Brentwood, Mt Juliet, Hendersonville, Lebanon, Nolensville, Smyrna, Gallatin, Clarksville, and surrounding counties.

Who is the public point of contact?

Kayla Brown, CEO and Principal Broker. Email info@buyunrepped.com.

What if I need to cancel, or want a refund?

Fees are subject to our cancellation and refund policy. Email info@buyunrepped.com if you need to cancel. Full terms are in the Terms of Use under "Fees / Cancellation / Refunds."
